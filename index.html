OFFICIAL ANNOUNCEMENT

Our Deepest Apologies Regarding the Data Breach Incident

Dear Zenith Corp. users and partners,

We at Zenith Corp. offer our deepest apologies for the exposure of sensitive personal information associated with our partner platform, Aura Health. We understand the distress and loss of security this incident has caused.

As a company that values trust and customer safety, we regard this incident as a serious failure. Personal data is deeply private, and we recognize that this incident has undermined the trust you placed in us. We sincerely regret the impact this has had on our users and partners.

We take this matter seriously. Our leadership and technical teams are working with Aura Health to investigate the incident, address the security vulnerability, and strengthen data protection measures. We recognize our responsibility to help restore confidence through meaningful action, not words alone.

We are committed to communicating transparently and sharing further updates as our investigation progresses and verified information becomes available. If you have immediate concerns or require assistance, please contact our support team at zenithcorp@gmail.com.

Once again, we sincerely apologize for the distress this incident has caused. We remain committed to learning from this failure, improving our practices, and working to rebuild your trust.

Sincerely,

Zenith Corp.
